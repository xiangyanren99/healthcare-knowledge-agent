# Missed Visit Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Community Care
**Policy ID:** OPS-MV-104
**Effective date:** January 15, 2026
**Document owner:** Clinical Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines the operational steps staff must follow when a scheduled home visit cannot be completed. Its purpose is to support timely communication, accurate documentation, and appropriate follow-up.

## Definition of a missed visit

A visit is considered missed when:

* The clinician cannot reach the patient at the scheduled location.
* The patient or caregiver cancels after the clinician has begun traveling.
* The clinician cannot complete the visit because access to the residence is unavailable.
* The visit cannot occur because of severe weather, transportation problems, or another operational barrier.
* The clinician is unable to attend and no qualified replacement is available.

A visit rescheduled and completed during the original scheduled service window is not considered missed.

## Required actions

The clinician must notify the designated clinical supervisor within **two hours of the scheduled visit time**. If the supervisor is unavailable, the clinician must contact the on-call operations coordinator.

The clinician must document:

* The scheduled date and time.
* The reason the visit was not completed.
* Attempts made to contact the patient or caregiver.
* Whether the supervisor was notified.
* Any immediate concerns reported by the patient or caregiver.
* The proposed rescheduling plan.

The clinician must not include unnecessary personal information in operational support messages.

## Rescheduling

The clinician should offer the earliest reasonable replacement time based on the patient’s service plan and staff availability. The patient or caregiver must confirm the new appointment. If contact cannot be established, the clinician must record each attempt and notify the supervisor.

## Repeated missed visits

Three missed visits within a rolling 30-day period require a supervisor review. The supervisor should evaluate communication barriers, scheduling issues, transportation concerns, caregiver availability, and whether the service plan requires reassessment.

The clinician must not independently discontinue services or change the care plan.

## Questions this policy does not answer

This policy does not determine:

* Whether a patient needs emergency assistance.
* Whether services should be discontinued.
* Whether a clinical intervention is required.
* How billing should be handled.
* Whether a missed visit satisfies external regulatory requirements.

Those questions must be escalated to the appropriate clinical, compliance, or billing representative.
