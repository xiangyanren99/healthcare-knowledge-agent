# New Clinician Onboarding Guide

**Organization:** Northstar Community Care
**Guide ID:** ONB-CLN-115
**Audience:** Newly hired field clinicians
**Document owner:** Workforce Education Team

## Purpose

This guide outlines the technology and workflow activities new clinicians should complete during their first week. Completion of onboarding does not authorize staff to perform work outside their assigned responsibilities or professional scope.

## First-week setup tasks

During the first working day, each clinician should:

* Activate their organizational account.
* Enroll in multifactor authentication.
* Confirm access to organizational email.
* Set up the approved mobile device.
* Install Northstar Visit Mobile.
* Review acceptable-use and device-security requirements.
* Confirm contact details for their supervisor and support team.

Passwords must not be shared with coworkers, supervisors, or support personnel.

## Required training

New clinicians must complete:

* Information privacy and security training.
* Mobile application orientation.
* Visit documentation workflow training.
* Missed-visit procedure training.
* Clinical escalation policy review.
* Offline documentation training.

Training completion should be recorded in the learning portal.

## Mobile application access

The clinician should sign in to Northstar Visit Mobile while connected to a trusted network. The daily schedule, training patient, and application menu should be visible.

If access is unavailable, the clinician should verify the account used, confirm multifactor authentication, and contact support. Staff should not use another clinician’s account to continue onboarding.

## Offline workflow practice

Before completing field work independently, the clinician must perform a supervised offline practice exercise:

1. Prepare a fictional training visit for offline use.
2. Disable network connectivity.
3. Enter sample documentation.
4. Confirm the record shows **Pending Sync**.
5. Restore connectivity.
6. Confirm the record changes to **Synced**.

The fictional training record must not contain real patient information.

## Support process

For technical issues, the clinician should first review the onboarding troubleshooting guide. If the problem remains, the clinician should submit a support request containing:

* Device type.
* Application version.
* General issue description.
* Error message or code.
* Troubleshooting already completed.

The clinician should not include patient-identifying information unless specifically instructed through an approved secure process.

## Knowledge check

By the end of the first week, the clinician must complete a 10-question knowledge check and receive a score of at least **80 percent**. One retake is permitted after reviewing missed topics.

A supervisor must confirm completion of required training, mobile access, offline practice, and the knowledge check before approving independent field use.
